Hub Highlights & Wins

This month was full of energy, progress, and inspiring contributions across our Hub. Below are some of the key wins that showcase the momentum we're building together.

Global Shapers at the Fulbright Association Annual Conference 2026

The Washington, DC Global Shapers Hub was represented at the Fulbright Association's Annual Conference through a presentation exploring the World Economic Forum's Reflective Leadership for Rebuilding Trust and its Leadership Commitment Framework. The presentation connected the Forum's approach to reflective leadership, accountability, and trust-building with the Fulbright community's commitment to international exchange and cross-cultural understanding. The discussion underscored the importance of developing leaders who can navigate complexity, strengthen institutions, and translate shared values into meaningful action.

Party at the Polls

This year, Global Shapers DC joined and supported Parties at the Polls across the US. Party at the Polls is an evidence-based, national, nonpartisan initiative to increase voter participation in underrepresented communities. GSDC hosted a party and community canvass, giving citizens information on the first-ever general election in DC with ranked choice voting and non-citizen voting.

 

Leadership Institute Welcomes Former CIA Executive Carmen Medina

On November 5, the Washington, DC Global Shapers Hub hosted Carmen Medina, former Deputy Director of Intelligence at the CIA, for a special Leadership Institute session at the Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Library. Bringing together 30 emerging leaders, the conversation explored the importance of sound judgment, strategic thinking, questioning assumptions, and leading through complexity. Drawing on her distinguished career in intelligence and national security, Medina challenged participants to embrace diverse perspectives, think critically, and approach leadership with curiosity and intention.

A Special Evening with World Economic Forum Young Global Leaders

During the November Hub meeting, the Washington, DC Global Shapers Hub convened a special conversation featuring three distinguished World Economic Forum Young Global Leaders: Elaine Genser of the United Nations, Farhan Latif of the El-Hibri Foundation, and Nilmini Rubin of Hedera. The gathering brought established global leaders into conversation with emerging changemakers, offering an opportunity to explore leadership journeys, lessons learned, and the responsibilities of shaping a more inclusive and resilient future. Through candid reflections and audience engagement, the evening strengthened connections across generations of leadership and highlighted the value of the World Economic Forum's global community.

New Shapers Induction Ceremony

We officially welcomed our newest Shapers into the Global Shapers community. Certificates in hand, they took their first step as members of the DC Hub, and we can't wait to see the energy, ideas, and impact they'll bring. Welcome to the Hub!

Diplomatic Dinner

For the third evening in the Diplomatic Dinner Series, DC Shapers sat down with Martin Thummel, Deputy Chief of Mission with the German Embassy. Together they discussed opportunities for youth to succeed in the new-age economy, the future of democracy, and Martin's expertise working and living in China.

Shred the Debt Fundraiser

On November 16, the Global Shapers DC Hub hosted a fundraiser for Shred the Debt, a national campaign to erase medical debt, in partnership with Undue Medical Debt and Chipotle. Every $1 raised allows us to relieve $100 of medical debt. Individual contributions and corporate matching partnerships have already helped relieve more than $150,000 in medical debt, and every dollar raised brings that relief to more DMV families. This holiday season, help us bring joy into the lives of families and reach the quarter-million debt relief mark.
